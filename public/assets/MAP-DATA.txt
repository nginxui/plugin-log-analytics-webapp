Map data of the log analytics dashboard

Country and region outlines: Natural Earth, public domain.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/

Region outlines of Bahamas, Bangladesh, Burundi, Guinea, Kenya, Latvia,
Lebanon, Libya, Micronesia, Moldova, Nepal, Philippines, Qatar, Saint Lucia,
Solomon Islands and Sudan: geoBoundaries, gbOpen, under public domain, CC0,
CC BY 4.0 or CC BY 3.0 IGO, as listed per country in the source repository
(scripts/admin1-geoboundaries-sources.json). Outlines simplified.
Runfola, D. et al. (2020) geoBoundaries: A global database of political
administrative boundaries. PLoS ONE 15(4): e0231866.
https://www.geoboundaries.org/

Region names: Wikidata, CC0. https://www.wikidata.org/

City names in the languages of the page: Wikidata, CC0, matched by GeoNames
id, and the alternate names of GeoNames, CC BY 4.0, where Wikidata has none.
https://www.wikidata.org/
GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org/), licensed under CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Names selected per language.
Traditional Chinese names neither source has are converted from the
simplified ones with OpenCC (Apache License 2.0).
https://github.com/BYVoid/OpenCC
